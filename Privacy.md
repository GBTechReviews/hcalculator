HCalculator 隐私政策

最后更新：2026年8月4日

HCalculator 不会收集、存储或传输任何个人数据。

• 无需创建账户或登录。
• 不使用任何分析、跟踪或广告 SDK。
• 本应用不会访问互联网。
• 计算历史记录和设置（主题、强调色、触觉反馈、动画）仅存储在您的设备上，绝不会离开您的设备。
• VIBRATE 权限仅用于按键时的触觉反馈。

清除应用数据或卸载应用将删除所有已存储的历史记录和设置。

联系方式：请在 [https://github.com/GBTechReviews/hcalculator/issues](https://github.com/GBTechReviews/hcalculator/issues) 提交问题。



# HCalculator Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 August 2026

HCalculator does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

- No account or sign-in is required.
- No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs are used.
- The app does not access the internet.
- Calculation history and settings (theme, accent colour, haptics,
  animations) are stored only on your device and never leave it.
- The VIBRATE permission is used solely for haptic feedback on key
  presses.

Clearing the app's data or uninstalling removes all stored history and
settings.

Contact: open an issue at
https://github.com/GBTechReviews/hcalculator/issues
